ACADEMIC REGULATIONS - ATTENDANCE AND EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY (Sample)

1. Minimum Attendance. A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course
to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

2. Condonation. Students with attendance between 65% and 74.99% may apply for
condonation with a valid reason (e.g., medical certificate) and payment of the
prescribed condonation fee. Condonation is granted at the discretion of the Head of Department.

3. Detention. Students with attendance below 65% in a course are detained in that course
and must repeat it.

4. Exam passing. A student must score at least 40% in the end-semester exam and 40% overall to pass a course.
